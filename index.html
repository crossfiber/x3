The method

Where clubhead speed actually comes from

It comes from the ground. You push into the floor, the floor pushes back, and that force runs up through your hips and your thorax into the club. Every mile per hour you are missing leaks somewhere on that trip. A range lesson can see the top of the chain. It cannot see the bottom.

What the hour looks like

What happens in the hour

Most coaching gives you a swing thought and sends you home to a body that cannot hold it. Sixty minutes, and it never ends on a guess.

  • 0:00

    Measure

    Baseline on the monitor and, on your first visit, on the force plates. We write down where you are before we touch anything.

  • 0:10

    Find the leak

    Swing work against the numbers, not against a video of somebody else. The monitor decides whether a change is real.

  • 0:40

    Build the body that holds it

    Golf specific strength aimed at the exact restriction the swing just exposed. Twenty minutes, in the weight room, with the coach who saw the swing.

  • 0:55

    Prove it

    Back on the monitor. Numbers from the start of the hour, numbers from the end, side by side. You leave knowing whether today worked.

You walk out with the card. Not a feeling about the session, a record of it, and the same card gets filled in again the following visit.

Before you book

I am 62 and I am not trying to become an athlete. Is this for me?

Yes, and the force plates matter more for you, not less. Speed loss after 50 is usually a rate of force problem, not a flexibility problem, and it is the thing a range lesson has no way to see. The strength work is scaled to what your body will actually tolerate, which is exactly what having a physical therapy team in the same building is for.

I already have a coach I like. Am I supposed to leave him?

No. Plenty of golfers here keep their swing coach and use this for the speed and body side. Bring your coach's notes and we will work with them rather than around them. If a change we make contradicts something he is teaching, you will hear that from us, out loud, in the session.

What if I am currently hurt?

Then say so when you book. X3 runs a full physical therapy practice on the same floor, and the honest answer some weeks is that you need Andrew's table before you need my launch monitor. We will tell you which one you need. A session sold to a golfer who should be in rehab is a session that costs us the next twelve.
